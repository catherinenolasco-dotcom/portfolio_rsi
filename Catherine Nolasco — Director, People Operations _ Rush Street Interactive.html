Compensation Program Management
How I run a full compensation program cycle
From philosophy to benchmarking to merit cycle execution — the methodology behind the metrics.

📊 Benchmarking Methodology

I use multiple data sources to triangulate market rates — never relying on a single survey. Primary sources include Radford Global Technology Survey, Mercer Total Remuneration Survey, and Willis Towers Watson (WTW) Salary Budget Survey. I map internal titles to survey job codes by scope and complexity, establish pay ranges targeting the 50th–75th percentile, and calculate compa-ratios for every employee to surface compression, inversion, or equity gaps before the merit cycle opens.

RadfordMercerWTWLevels.fyiCompa-Ratio AnalysisPay Range Design

🔄 Annual Merit Cycle

I run merit cycles on a Q3-benchmark → Q4-calibration → Jan-effective cadence. I build merit increase matrices calibrated by performance rating and compa-ratio, partner with Finance on budget allocation by department, run manager calibration sessions to ensure equity and consistency, secure CPO and leadership approvals, and prepare manager toolkits for delivering pay conversations. Every cycle includes a retroactive equity review before increases are finalized.

Merit Matrix DesignFinance PartnershipManager CalibrationPay Equity Review

🎯 Incentive Program Design

I design STI programs with three principles: line of sight (employees can connect their work to the metrics), meaningful differentiation (0–200% range), and simplicity (no more than 3–4 metrics per role). I set bonus targets by level, define company vs. individual metric weighting, coordinate with Finance on accrual and payout execution, and update program design annually to stay aligned with evolving business priorities.

STI DesignBonus Target SettingMetric WeightingFinance Coordination

📋 Job Leveling & Career Architecture

I maintain a 6-level framework applicable across all job families, defined by scope, complexity, autonomy, and impact — not tenure. Each level has a corresponding compensation band (base range, bonus target, equity range) benchmarked annually. I conduct annual equity reviews using compa-ratio analysis to identify and correct compression, inversion, or pay disparities before they create retention or legal risk — and partner with Finance on band adjustments before any merit cycle opens.
